Psyconomics: trader behaviour lab

A simulated market of 8 real Indian companies and one gold fund, with news, rumours, crowd sentiment, spreads, fees, and four crisis events. The crises are different every game. Trade for 90 days. Every decision is logged, checked for 10 patterns, and checked against how you handled each crisis.

Before you start

Training tool. This is a school research project by Manya Sharma. Results describe trading patterns, not you as a person. Enter the code the researcher gave you (for example P01). Do not enter your real name. Results from a simulation show tendencies and are not a diagnosis.

Real company names are used only to make the game feel familiar. All prices and events are simulated, and prices start at 100. Each stock reacts to news the way its sector tends to (banks to interest rates, airlines to oil, and so on). Stock profiles last updated . This is not investment advice.

Leave this blank for a fresh random market. Enter the same code as another player to trade the exact same market and compare results.

You begin with $5,000 cash and 10 shares each of TCS, Reliance and Hindustan Unilever. A $3,000 bonus arrives on day 31. Along the way, expect four crisis events, such as a market crash, a rate shock, an oil spike or a scandal at one company. Which ones you get, and when, is random. How likely are you to beat the market average (equal mix of all 8 holdings)?
